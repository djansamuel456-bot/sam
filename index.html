Who We Are

We Don't Just Print.
We Craft Impressions.

Printing Affairs was born from a simple but powerful belief: that every business, big or small, deserves print materials that truly represent the quality and character of their brand.

Based in Accra, Ghana, we started as a small workshop with big ambitions. Over the years, we've grown into a full-service printing house trusted by entrepreneurs, corporates, schools, NGOs, churches, and event organizers across the country. What hasn't changed is our commitment — to deliver vivid, durable, and impactful print products, on time, every time.

We invest in modern equipment and continuously train our team so that every order — whether it's a single business card or a thousand branded T-shirts — gets the same meticulous care and craftsmanship.

Quality First

We never compromise on materials or finish. Every job is inspected before it leaves our hands.

Client-Centred

We listen. We advise. We deliver exactly what you envisioned — or better.

Built in Ghana

Proudly Ghanaian, serving local businesses and helping brands shine across the country.

Reliability

Deadlines are sacred to us. When we say it'll be ready, it will be ready.

Our Mission

Empowering Brands Through Print

Our mission is to be the go-to print partner for every brand in Ghana — delivering premium quality, creative expertise, and reliable service that helps our clients stand out and make lasting impressions.
